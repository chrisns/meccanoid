06 / GROWN-UP SETUP

The robot
workshop.

The clever bits, all in one place.

Movement ranges measured. All eight joints were moved by hand through their comfortable travel on 19 September 2026. Automatic limits sit four raw units inside those endpoints.

Measure travel by hand

Start with a comfortable neutral stance and support the arms. Start capture requests the original app’s read mode and disables motion. First check that a joint moves freely; if it resists, cancel rather than force it. Slowly move each joint through its comfortable travel without pushing against hard stops. Readings update below; Finish saves joints travelled at least 10 units and adds a four-unit safety margin. The starting pose becomes neutral. Motion stays disabled afterward.

Ready to measure physical limits.

Calibration · mapping, limits and direction

All eight joint identities, neutral positions and comfortable travel were measured on this robot. Slot 6 turns the head; slot 7 tilts it sideways. Defaults include a four-unit safety margin. Changes save automatically. Reverse changes the camera direction for a joint.

Connecting from a Chromebook

Use Chrome, turn Bluetooth on, and connect one Chromebook at a time. Choose the device whose name starts with MECCANOID. Press the robot’s yellow button if it asks. A school-managed Chromebook may need an administrator to allow Bluetooth or the camera.
